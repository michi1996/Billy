Buddy sends your prompts from the Pebble phone app to the language model server you run yourself (for example llama.cpp at home behind Cloudflare Access). Enter its address and access token in the app settings. There is no Buddy cloud service, and nothing is sent to Google or other AI providers. Weather comes from Open-Meteo and place names are looked up with OpenStreetMap Nominatim. If you do not agree, do not use Buddy.
