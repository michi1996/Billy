# Buddy 0.1
Buddy is a fork of Billy and Bobby that talks to your own language model server.
## Your own server
Prompts go from the Pebble phone app to your llama.cpp server behind Cloudflare Access. Configure it in the app settings.
## Watch features
Alarms, timers, timeline reminders, settings, weather cards and clarification pickers work as before. Simple timers and alarms are set instantly without the model.
